Meet Jennifer

I believe there is gold in every person.

I'm Jennifer Jones, a Transformation & Life Purpose Coach, speaker, teacher, and strategist. Helping people recognize who they are, and what they carry, is the heart of my work.

My story

I know what it means to rebuild.

I've walked through trauma, abuse, and seasons where I nearly lost myself. I know what it feels like to be disconnected from who you are, to repeat patterns you don't understand, and to struggle to fully show up in your own life.

I carried generational wounds and lived without boundaries until I chose to break the cycle. That work changed how I see myself, and it deepened my compassion for women who have spent years surviving, performing, pleasing, or losing themselves in the expectations of others.

What happened to you matters. But it does not have to be the only story you live from.

Compassion, truth, strategy, and accountability.

I don't believe transformation happens through encouragement alone.

We look at what's happening beneath the surface, challenge the beliefs that keep you repeating the same choices, and rebuild your self trust. And when a vision begins to take shape, we build the plan.

Faith is the foundation of my life and work.

I am a Christian, and I believe God created every person with inherent worth, gifts, and purpose. My faith shapes how I approach people: with compassion, dignity, hope, and belief in their capacity to grow.

The Renewed Soul welcomes women from every background and belief. You don't have to share my faith to work with me. When faith matters to your journey, we make room for it. When it doesn't, that's welcome too.

I also help people build what they've been carrying.

I have a gift for organization, administration, and bringing ideas into focus. With more than 28 years in hospitality and corporate events and advisory work with early stage founders through the SBDC, my work often reaches into books, websites, brands, business strategy, and launches.
