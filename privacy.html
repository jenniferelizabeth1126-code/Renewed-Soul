Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

The Renewed Soul is a life purpose coaching business operated by Jennifer Jones. Our website is renewedsoul.co. You can reach us at jennifer@renewedsoul.co.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to give us, including your name, email address, phone number, and any details you share through our forms, our cohort application, or by email.

How we use your information

We use it to respond to your inquiry, review cohort applications, schedule discovery calls and coaching sessions, deliver services you've enrolled in, and send program updates and resources you've opted into.

What we do not do

We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing.

Third party services

Our website uses Calendly for booking calls, Google Forms for cohort applications, Stan Store for digital products and the free quiz, and Stripe for cohort payments. Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how it handles your data.

Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by emailing jennifer@renewedsoul.co. We'll respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted on this page.
